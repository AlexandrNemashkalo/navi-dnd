PROMPT TEMPLATE — SIMPLE READABLE FANTASY PIXEL ART

Generate ONE original asset for a fantasy terminal RPG. Final delivery will be
EXACTLY 32 columns by 32 rows, so design the entire image for this tiny canvas.
Subject: [one named object or character]. Purpose: [inventory / portrait / scene].

Composition: one subject, centered, complete silhouette, with a one-pixel clear
margin. For portraits show ONLY head and the top of shoulders. For objects show
the complete object at a clear side or three-quarter angle. Avoid foreshortening
that hides its identifying feature. No extra props or decorative scenery.

The intended style is classic hand-clustered fantasy pixel art, like the game's
32x32 avatar and inventory collections: dark one-pixel outline, warm directional
light from the upper left, compact shadows, clean stepped diagonals and large
flat color clusters. Prefer 12-24 colors; three tones per main material are enough.
Keep the character's expression calm and readable. Do not make a mascot or emoji.

The silhouette and three identifying features must remain understandable at 1x.
Reserve enough pixels for each identifying feature. A portrait eye needs a dark
socket and a bright pixel; a handle or opening needs a clear gap. Use broad shapes
instead of scales, stitches, engraved filigree or individually rendered hairs.
Describe metal with two highlight clusters, leather with two or three broad tones.

If the output must be larger than 32x32, render an enlarged view of a conceptual
32x32 sprite: each logical pixel is the same square size, aligned to one grid.
Do not draw a pixel grid. Do not simulate pixel art with differently sized blocks.
No gradients, antialiasing, blur, dithering, tiny texture, bevels, glow, particles,
out-of-focus shading, text, watermark, UI frame, button border or cast ground shadow.
Use genuine alpha transparency, never a painted checkerboard or black backdrop.

Portrait-specific: keep horns, ears and hair within the canvas. Face occupies most
of the upper 24 rows; a simple collar occupies the last 5-7 rows. Do not draw a
full-body actor. All essential facial features must be several logical pixels wide.

LOCAL DELIVERY CONTRACT (performed by the build script, not guaranteed by a prompt):
Keep the generator output as an archived source. Prepare a separate RGBA PNG whose
actual dimensions are 32x32 using nearest-neighbor sampling and binary alpha.
Never stretch the aspect ratio. Inspect at native size and integer enlargement.
Do not indiscriminately quantize: it can erase rare but essential eye highlights.
Reject or regenerate images with unreadable features or broken silhouettes.
Record source dimensions, generation prompt, transform and hashes. Only the final
32x32 PNG enters the gallery/runtime. Enlarged previews are NOT production assets.
