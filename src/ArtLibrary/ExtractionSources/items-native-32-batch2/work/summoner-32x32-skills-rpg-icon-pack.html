Summoner 32×32 Skills RPG Icon Pack

Description

Enter the mystical world of the arcane arts with our Summoner 32×32 Skills RPG Icon Pack. This carefully crafted collection brings a variety of summoning abilities to life, giving 2D game developers a wealth of options to implement in their game projects. Here you will find 48 icons, each prepared in a resolution of 32×32. As a result, you can realize their potential in any of the popular genres (runners, adventures, quests, turn-based strategies, RPGs, MMOs, card games, fantasy, sidescrollers, and others).

The repository consists of the following abilities: summon scroll, book of spells, summon fire fox, claw strike, dragon’s breath, snake’s shackles, protective barrier, enhancing attack with the beast, scout beetle, mind reading, summoner’s staff and many other useful icons. They will help you fill your world with new gameplay and give your players the opportunity to decide for themselves in which branch of events they want to develop – to become a sorcerer or a mysterious summoner. If you wish, you can easily bring your ideas to life using any graphic editor, like Adobe Photoshop.

  • Graphics: 2D, Pixel Art;
  • Quantity: 48 pieces;
  • Files: PSD, PNG;
  • Resolution: 32×32.
